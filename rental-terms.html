Reservations

Submitting a request through this website does not create a reservation and does not guarantee availability. A reservation is confirmed only after we approve the request, payment is received, and the rental agreement is signed.

Driver eligibility

  • A valid, unexpired driver's license is required
  • Minimum driver age: [specify age]
  • Proof of full-coverage insurance, or purchase of an approved coverage option, is required
  • Additional verification may apply, which may include a driving record review
  • Only approved drivers named on the rental agreement may operate the vehicle

Security deposit

A refundable security deposit may be required depending on the vehicle and rental details. The amount, hold method, and release timeline are confirmed before the rental begins. [Specify amounts, hold method, and release timeline.]

Vehicle use

  • No smoking or vaping in any vehicle
  • Mileage limits may apply; overage is billed at [rate] per mile
  • No racing, track use, off-road use, towing, or commercial transport of passengers
  • The vehicle may not be sublet, loaned, or taken outside the permitted area without written approval
  • Fuel level and cleanliness must match the condition at pickup; otherwise fees may apply
  • All traffic citations, tolls, and parking violations remain the renter's responsibility
