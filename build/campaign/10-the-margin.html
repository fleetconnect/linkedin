margin, n.

1. The blank space around the printed area of a page. Traditionally the most generous element of a well-set book, and the only one the reader never notices.

2. In freight: the difference between a lane at $1.80 and the same lane at $2.40. Not a function of effort, hours, equipment or work ethic. A function of whether anybody was negotiating.

Everything on this page is margin except the part that matters. Most owner-operator profit-and-loss statements are set the same way — and the block keeps getting smaller.
