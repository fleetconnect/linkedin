Adverse reactions
  • Sleeping through the night
  • Answering roughly forty fewer calls per week
  • Sudden and total loss of interest in load boards
  • Weekends occurring on weekends
  • Difficulty recalling what a carrier packet looks like
  • Your child recognising your voice on the phone
  • In rare cases, enjoying the business again
Mechanism of action
DISPATCH™ acts by removing freight sourcing, rate negotiation and administrative burden from the operator and relocating it to a party who performs these functions on a full-time basis. Onset of effect is typically observed within one billing cycle. Patients commonly report a measurable reduction in unplanned deadhead and an increase in the predictability of weekly gross.
Contraindications
Not indicated for operators who enjoy negotiating at 11pm, or who consider a $1.80 lane a fair outcome.

How supplied
Supplied as one (1) dedicated dispatcher per carrier. Not available as software, as a load board subscription, or as a shared queue. Each unit includes rate negotiation, lane planning, rate confirmations, check calls and carrier packet handling.
Storage & handling
Store in a working relationship. Effect diminishes if the operator continues to answer broker calls directly.
Patient counselling
Advise the operator that improvement is measured in predictability, not peak weeks. Instruct to report any week that cannot be planned by the preceding Friday.
WarningDiscontinuation may result in the immediate return of broker calls, unplanned empty miles, and a weekly gross that varies by more than fifty percent. Do not operate a load board while experiencing fatigue.
