# Fleet photography — provenance and licensing

**These are interim placeholder photographs. They are not photographs of Rich
Exotic Rentals vehicles.** Replace them with photos of your own fleet before you
drive traffic to this site — see README.md § "Vehicle photography".

## Why that matters

A rental listing implies the customer gets *that* vehicle. These images show
other people's cars, in other countries, in colours that may not match your
fleet. Every registration plate visible in the source images has been blurred,
because publishing a stranger's plate on a commercial site is not something you
want to be doing.

## Processing applied

Each image was: registration plate blurred → cropped to remove surrounding
clutter → resized → graded toward the dark gold-on-black brand treatment
(saturation reduced, contrast raised, exposure lowered, highlights warmed, and a
vignette added on the card images so the frame falls off into the page).
